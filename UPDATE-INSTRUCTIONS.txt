DAVE AND CONTROLS

  direct crop of Dave from comic page 3. His face is NOT regenerated.

  twelve seconds. Guidance text remains until the lesson is completed.
- Existing agreed dialogue only; no added tutorial dialogue.
- Worn metal console, recessed weapon controls, mechanical stick surrounds.
- Red EJECT cap, clear label, hazard stripes and five-second hold-progress rim.
- Existing control positions, weapon mapping and touch targets retained.

EJECTION AND MENU AUDIO
- Manual and automatic ejection use the approved five-second countdown.
- Accelerating warning beeps replace the klaxon and match the red flashes.
- Carousel scrolling and stopping are silent; its whirr is no longer loaded.
- Distinct hardpoint and weapon-confirmation beeps retained.

THE BREAKERS YARD
- First combat arena: 1340 x 2015 world units, approximately 25% more
  total ground area than the 1200 x 1800 tutorial. Mech scale unchanged.
- Dark warm-charcoal ground, scrap and containers, two opaque overhead
  gantries with 45-foot clearance. Only ground bases and scrap block movement.
- One enemy mech and three smaller attack rovers. Each rover has exactly
  one independently rotating cannon, using the approved rover artwork.
- Enemy navigation routes around obstructions. AI phases allocate at most
  two simultaneous control actions: move/aim, aim/left-fire, move/right-fire,
  or two fire controls. A firing thumb cannot also adjust aim.
- Training enemies also patrol and aim; tutorial shooting remains limited
  to the shield exercise. Combat enemies do not respawn.
- Complete the tutorial and return to the workshop; the next DEPLOY enters
  The Breakers Yard. Clearing all enemies shows victory; ejection recovers
  the pilot and returns to the workshop after escape.
